On your last day at the world's last pneumatic post office, you discover the tubes reach further than any map: across cities, across centuries. Match the objects on your desk to the letters in your hand, turn the dials to the right year, and send them off.

![The desk, the letters, and the tubes](/assets/images/pipe-dream-desk.webp)

[Play it on itch.io](https://dracco-0n.itch.io/pipe-dream) (browser, Windows, macOS)

Made in 72 hours for the Ludum Dare 60 Jam (theme: *Old Tech*) with space_yoga (design, writing), Spring (design, art) and The Tourist (music). I did the programming and shared the design and writing.
