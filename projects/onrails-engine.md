*OnRails Engine* is my MSc dissertation project at UCL (COMP0073), developed with Nimesh Patel for MotionInput Games and supervised by Prof. Dean Mohamedally. It is an offline, mixed-initiative procedural content generation system that helps children, including those with motor impairments, create their own on-rails shooter levels. You describe a level in a sentence, and the engine turns it into something you can actually play.

Everything runs locally on an ordinary laptop, with no cloud dependency.
